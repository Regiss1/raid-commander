## Reason for revision

The requester observed that combat appears to stop once a protected boss reaches one health. The current resolver clamps damage to remaining removable health, then suppresses events at zero effective damage. Ranged attacks consequently return `NoEffect` and no tracer is emitted. Party brains remain in their fighting state; they are not becoming idle. T7's recorded baseline and verification explicitly expect zero damage and no outcome at the health floor.

The requested behavior changes that baseline: successful hits must keep reporting their normal damage while protection changes only the boss's remaining health. The requested attack-speed stat is also new scope; current party actions use a fixed 1.5-second component interval, and combat stats have no attack-speed value. Record both changes as one bounded scope revision rather than rewriting T7's original acceptance baseline.

## Objective

Keep valid party attacks, normal damage events/logging and ranged feedback active against a protected boss at one health, and make automated party attack frequency derive from a configurable attacks-per-second stat, initialized to 1 for all four party members.

## Scope changes

### Protected boss combat

- Keep boss protection off by default. With protection enabled, successful attacks resolve and report the damage they would deal before the development health floor. Apply the floor only to the boss's resulting health.
- For example, a valid 20-damage hit on a protected boss at five health reports 20 damage and leaves one health; subsequent valid 20-damage hits continue reporting 20 while health remains one. Do not emit a boss death event or put the party into post-death idle behavior.
- Use the existing combat event/log flow, stable IDs, sequencing, timestamps and ranged-hit presentation. Protected ranged hits count as successful actions and continue producing tracers at the normal automated attack cadence.
- Keep protected debug damage/kill health clamping consistent. Update action-result/debug feedback where it currently infers reported damage solely from health loss.
- Disabling protection restores existing effective-damage and death behavior. Do not change damage reporting for ordinary unprotected combat, revive a dead boss, or log failed out-of-range/blocked/dead-source requests as successful hits.

### Attack-speed stats

- Add a data-driven attacks-per-second stat to combat stats and expose it through the combat unit, including a valid fallback for units without a stats asset. Initialize Tank, Healer, Melee DPS and Ranged DPS to 1 attack per second. Preserve unrelated stats, asset identity and existing scene customization.
- Automated attacks use an interval of `1 / attackSpeed`: 1 means one attack per second, 2 means one every 0.5 seconds, and 0.5 means one every two seconds. Validate invalid values so timing remains finite and safe.
- Attack speed controls automated attacks only. The current Healer has no offensive attack; it still receives the stat at 1 for data consistency, while its existing basic healing interval remains separate at 1.5 seconds. Do not add a healer damage action.
- Preserve the stationary boss's existing two-second attack interval. This revision does not migrate boss timing to the party stat.
- Debug attack/heal buttons remain immediate and do not acquire an automated attack-speed cooldown. Trusted direct damage/kill/reset tools also remain immediate.
- Keep action timing separate from the throttled role/position decisions so the 0.2-second decision interval does not cap or materially distort configurable attack rates. Preserve normal validity checks and avoid banked bursts after failed actions, loss of sight/range, manual movement or reset.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With boss protection on, run multiple normal automated melee/ranged attacks after boss health reaches one. Every successful hit produces the normal positive damage event/log amount at its configured rate, the boss remains alive at one, ranged tracers continue, and no boss death event or post-death AI transition occurs.
2. Verify the protected threshold-crossing example (five health, 20 damage: report 20, retain one health). Verify no duplicate damage events and unchanged event metadata/order.
3. Turn protection off and verify the next eligible lethal hit kills the boss normally, with existing effective damage/death logging and party idle behavior. Protection must not revive an already dead boss.
4. All four party stats assets and unit fallbacks expose the attack-speed stat with an initial value of 1. Existing stat/scene edits and asset references survive setup reapplication.
5. Against a living, visible, in-range target, Tank, Melee DPS and Ranged DPS sustain one automated attack per second within player-frame tolerance. Focused timing checks with 0.5 and 2 attacks per second prove the value changes cadence, rather than acting as a fixed one-second constant. Invalid values cannot cause division errors or uncontrolled attack loops.
6. Healer's stat is 1, but its existing basic healing stays on the separate 1.5-second interval. Boss remains stationary with a two-second melee interval. Neither acquires the new party attack cadence.
7. Repeated debug attack/heal button requests remain immediate, independently of automated attack cooldowns. Invalid actions still respect existing range, sight, faction and alive-state checks and produce no success events.
8. Recheck manual movement/combat/resume, sight loss/recovery, selection independence, death/reset, an unattended unprotected encounter, reactive health/event logging, and setup idempotency. Validate the protected ranged tracer visually in the actual Game view and confirm compilation/Console results.

## Out of scope

Boss attack-speed migration; changing healing cadence or adding healer attacks; shared normal/debug cooldown enforcement; haste/buffs, casts, projectiles, threat, formations, new boss mechanics/abilities, UI stat editors, encounter result/restart screens, and unrelated health-bar sizing changes.

- Extend existing setup paths and focused Play-mode verification. Update the dedicated combat and autonomous-AI system notes only when implementation exists; their current implemented descriptions remain the T7 baseline during planning.
