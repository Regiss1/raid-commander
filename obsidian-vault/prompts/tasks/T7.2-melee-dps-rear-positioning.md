## Objective

During ordinary autonomous combat, make Melee DPS seek a safe, reachable position in the boss's rear sector near its own maximum melee range. Fall back to the closest reachable side and then the front, while preserving player orders, Awareness-based escape, and existing retreat toward Tank.

## Reason for revision and current baseline

T7 implements approaching and attacking within range, without a rear-position requirement. This is an extension, not a claim that T7 failed its original acceptance criteria.

Source inspection confirms that `PartyRoleBrain` currently holds any position with clear sight and sufficient melee range. Its ordinary melee search prefers 75% of the unit's melee range. `CombatPositionSearch` samples 48 candidates on three rings, validates complete NavMesh paths, line of sight, range and pending-hazard safety, then ranks candidates by path length and spacing without considering boss facing.

T8 supplies boss facing, hazard-aware movement and retreat toward a living Tank when the non-casting boss actively chases a non-Tank unit. T10 gates committed escape through Awareness checks. Valid combat actions already run independently of positioning. Unit bodies are mutually passable by project rule.

## Confirmed scope

- Change only Melee DPS ordinary autonomous positioning. Define rear/side/front relative to the boss's current facing on the XZ plane, with elevation resolved from navigation.
- Use a rear sector, rather than demanding the exact point directly behind the boss. Hold an acceptable position; reevaluate when boss movement/facing or route validity makes it unacceptable. Avoid repeated side switching or movement oscillation under stable conditions.
- Prefer near the unit's own maximum melee range, allowing a small stopping tolerance inside the valid range. Existing center-to-center XZ combat range and line-of-sight checks remain authoritative; do not change combat stats or range semantics.
- Prefer valid rear positions. If terrain, navigation, sight or existing hazard-safety rules prevent rear access, choose the reachable side with the shortest safe NavMesh route. If neither rear nor side offers a valid position, permit a valid front position. A pending hazard must not be crossed merely to achieve a higher positional preference.
- Prefer destinations outside the boss and Tank body footprints. Also prefer safe routes around those bodies when feasible. This is a soft autonomous route preference: retain mutual passability, disabled unit avoidance/separation and existing collision exclusions. Crossing bodies remains permitted when needed; do not make unit bodies solid navigation obstacles.
- Route and destination overlap avoidance is subordinate to manual orders, successful Awareness escape, and existing Tank retreat. It must not delay, replace or prevent those behaviors, even when they require overlapping or crossing a body.
- Preserve priority: death/inactive encounter, manual movement, committed Awareness escape, existing chase retreat toward Tank, then ordinary positioning. Preserve the existing retreat trigger; this task does not expand it to every boss-targeting or casting state.
- Preserve failed Awareness-check behavior and preventive ordinary path avoidance. Do not introduce guaranteed detection, extra rolls or a new SAFE state.
- Continue attacks whenever existing range, sight and cooldown requirements allow, including while repositioning. Resume ordinary positioning after higher-priority movement releases ownership.
- If no valid combat position is available, retain predictable failure/retry behavior without acquiring manual ownership, reporting false arrival, or looping movement commands without progress.

## Implementation boundaries and tuning

Keep decision production in the Units feature and execution in `UnitMovement`; selection must not own positioning. Reuse the existing encounter references, hazard queries and combat checks. Any search extension must preserve other callers' behavior, including boss and other-role positioning.

Rear-sector width, stopping tolerance, body-clearance margin and stability thresholds have no requester-specified numeric values. Keep relevant new values explicit and tunable; choose and report initial values during implementation, verifying them against this task's observable behavior. Do not reinterpret these tuning details as authorization to add mechanics or change combat balance. Ask before any choice materially changes the confirmed scope.

The current bounded search cannot prove global unreachability in arbitrary NavMeshes. Cover rear and both side regions deliberately and verify fallback in controlled layouts with known reachable and blocked positions; do not claim exhaustive path-planning guarantees. Preserve complete-path validation and existing safe-path filtering.

Integrate required configuration through repeatable current-scene setup without regenerating arena geometry or overwriting authored tuning. Update the dedicated autonomous-party-AI system note and its revision table when the behavior is actually implemented; update other system notes only if their systems change.

## Acceptance criteria

1. In an open, safe arena, Melee DPS starting in front or beside the boss moves into its rear sector near its own maximum melee range and continues valid attacks. An already acceptable rear position is held under stable conditions.
2. With rear access blocked by a solid obstacle or impassable terrain, Melee DPS uses a valid side position. In a fixture with unequal safe side-route lengths, it chooses the shorter reachable route.
3. With rear and both sides unavailable but a valid front position available, Melee DPS can fight from the front without repeatedly issuing impossible rear/side moves. Removing the obstruction allows it to seek the rear again.
4. Boss movement or rotation that invalidates the current sector/spacing causes reevaluation, without continuous movement under an unchanged valid configuration.
5. Ordinary destinations remain within the unit's own melee range, have clear sight and complete navigation paths, and preserve existing pending-hazard safety. A shorter hazardous route never wins over a valid safe route.
6. When safe alternatives exist, the chosen destination does not overlap Tank/boss bodies and the preferred approach goes around their footprints. When such avoidance is infeasible, unit passability remains intact and no physical pushing or blocking is introduced.
7. Player movement immediately retains priority, including orders into danger or through unit bodies. Completion, cancellation, interruption and failure retain existing ownership semantics, and ordinary positioning resumes when manual ownership ends.
8. Scripted successful Awareness rolls interrupt ordinary rear/overlap positioning for committed escape. Failed rolls preserve the current check cadence and preventive path rules; blocked exits retain existing retry behavior.
9. The existing active-chase retreat toward a living Tank wins over rear positioning and body avoidance. Neither a desired rear destination nor an overlap preference delays this retreat.
10. No-valid-position, boss death/inactivity, unit death and session reset preserve predictable stop/retry/recovery behavior. Tank, Healer and Ranged DPS positioning and all combat timing remain unchanged.
11. Reapplying setup preserves authored values and geometry without duplicate components. The saved scene can exercise the behavior without manual rewiring.

## Implementation and verification result (2026-10-22)

- `MeleeDpsPositionSearch` adds a Melee-only bounded rear/side/front search with complete-path, sight, melee-range and pending-hazard checks. Boss/Tank body footprints affect destination and approach preference only; unit collision and NavMesh passability are unchanged. `PartyRoleBrain` retains manual, committed Awareness and active-chase retreat priority over this ordinary positioning.
- Initial serialized tuning: rear half-angle 60 degrees, maximum-range inset 0.35 units, rear hold tolerance 0.3 units, body margin 0.15 units and repath threshold 0.6 units. The saved arena contains these fields; repeated setup preserved a custom 57-degree angle and created no duplicate wiring.
- Unity 6000.6.6f1 compiled. The focused advancing-frame Play-mode suite passed open rear approach/hold, attacks, rotation, rear/side blockage, asymmetric shorter-side choice, front fallback, restored access, no-sight failure, boss-body detour, manual cancellation/resume, failed/successful Awareness and active-chase retreat. The actual Game view showed the Melee DPS behind the boss while attacks continued.
- Existing Party AI and Awareness Play-mode regressions passed. The boss fast suite passed after its precondition was aligned with the already-authored two-second cast. The existing movement runner could not register its synthetic right-click action in this Editor session, although it logged the expected ground hit; focused movement ownership checks through `UnitMovement` passed. This input-runner limitation remains unverified for T7.2.
- A later optional recompile request timed out while Unity's main thread was unresponsive. The intervening source optimization was reverted, leaving the previously compiled and verified implementation. No later Play-mode result is claimed.
- The search is bounded and does not prove global reachability on arbitrary NavMeshes. Focused blocker fixtures use sight obstruction without a navigation rebake. The arena, source, test, and vault changes formed the uncommitted review checkpoint approved by the requester on 2026-10-23.
