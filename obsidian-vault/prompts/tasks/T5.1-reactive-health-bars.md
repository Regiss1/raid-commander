## Objective

Implement reactive health bar updates for every combat unit in our Unity 6, C#, 3D top-down MMO raid simulator.

Health bars must automatically reflect changes to their associated unit's health without polling health values every frame.

The game uses two distinct presentations:

- **Party members:** World-space health bars positioned above their 3D character models.
- **Boss:** A screen-space health bar anchored at the top of the screen.

Both presentations must share the same health notification mechanism.

Before implementing, inspect the existing project and reuse established components and conventions.

## 1. Health state notifications

Ensure the existing health component provides an event such as `HealthChanged`.

Requirements:

- Publish notifications whenever current or maximum health changes.
- Include sufficient information to calculate the updated health percentage.
- When maximum health changes during play, keep current health and clamp it to the new maximum; do not preserve its previous percentage or heal it automatically.
- Publish notifications only after applying the health change.
- Ensure damage, healing, and health resets use the existing health system.
- Do not introduce a second source of truth for health.

Reuse existing health-change events where available.

## 2. Reactive health bar components

Implement or extend the existing health bar components to:

- Subscribe to their associated unit's health-change notifications.
- Initialize displays using the unit's current health.
- Update immediately when health changes.
- Calculate fill percentage from current and maximum health.
- Clamp the displayed percentage between 0% and 100%.
- Display an empty bar when the unit dies.
- Update correctly after health resets.
- Unsubscribe from events when disabled or destroyed.

Do not use per-frame health polling.

Prefer shared health bar update logic with separate presentation components for party members and bosses.

## 3. Party health bars — World-space UI

Requirements:

- Display a health bar above each party member's 3D model.
- Maintain a configurable world-space offset.
- Follow the character's movement.
- Remain readable from the angled top-down camera.
- Orient toward the camera when necessary.
- Remain readable while zooming.
- Update each character independently.

Do not attach world-space health bars to bosses.

## 4. Boss health bar — Screen-space UI

Implement the boss health bar as part of the game's HUD.

### Position and layout

- Anchor the bar horizontally at the top of the screen.
- Keep it independent of the boss's world position and camera movement.
- Support different screen resolutions and aspect ratios.
- Display the boss's name below the bar.
- Display the **health percentage on the left side of the bar**.
- Display **current health / maximum health on the right side of the bar**.
- Align these values consistently within the health bar's layout.

Example:

**Raid Boss**

<escape>75% 7,500 / 10,000</escape>

### Health values and formatting

- Percentage must represent current health divided by maximum health.
- Display percentages as whole numbers (e.g., `75%`).
- Display current and maximum health as whole numbers with thousands separators (e.g., `7,500 / 10,000`).
- Use the same current and maximum health values that determine the bar's fill.
- Update percentage, numerical values, and fill together when health changes.
- Display `0%` and `0 / maximum health` when the boss dies.
- Handle zero maximum health safely.

### Behavior

- Subscribe to the boss's health-change notifications.
- Initialize correctly when the HUD is enabled.
- Update immediately after damage, healing, or health resets.
- Keep the health bar visible and correctly positioned during camera movement.
- Support one active boss for the MVP.

Do not implement multi-boss UI or phase indicators.

## 5. Text readability and outlines

All text introduced or modified by this task must have a visible **2-pixel outline**.

This includes:

- Boss name.
- Boss health percentage.
- Boss current/maximum health.
- Any party member names or numerical health values displayed in health bars.

Requirements:

- Prefer TextMeshPro for rendering text.
- Use a consistent outline style across both world-space and screen-space UI.
- The outline must provide sufficient contrast against varying arena backgrounds and health bar colors.
- Use a shared text style or material where practical.
- Ensure the outline appears approximately 2 screen pixels thick at the intended display scale, accounting for Canvas scaling and world-space text.
- Do not rely exclusively on shadows as a replacement for the outline.

Keep text readable during camera zoom and across supported screen resolutions.

## 6. Integration with combat events

The expected behavior is:

1. A unit receives damage or healing.
2. The combat system calculates and applies the result.
3. The health component publishes a health-change notification.
4. The corresponding health bar updates its fill and numerical information.
5. The combat logging system independently records the resolved outcome.

Health bars must not depend on `CombatLogService`.

Both presentation types must consume the same health state notification mechanism.

## 7. Testing

Use the existing combat debug actions from Task 5.

Verify:

- Damage decreases the corresponding health bar.
- Healing increases the corresponding health bar.
- Healing to maximum health fills the bar completely.
- Lethal damage empties the bar.
- Health resets correctly restore the bar.
- Changes to one character do not affect another's health bar.
- Disabling and re-enabling a bar restores its display correctly.
- Party health bars follow moving characters.
- Party health bars remain readable while zooming.
- Boss health bar remains anchored to the top of the screen.
- Boss percentage and current/maximum health update together.
- Displayed values match the actual combat unit health.
- Text outlines are visible and consistent.
- Boss HUD layout works across different resolutions and aspect ratios.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All party members have functional world-space health bars.
2. The boss has a functional screen-space health bar.
3. The boss has no duplicate world-space health bar.
4. Boss health percentage appears on the left.
5. Boss current/maximum health appears on the right.
6. All health values and bar fills remain synchronized.
7. All health bar UI text has a visible 2-pixel outline.
8. Health updates are event-driven, without per-frame polling.
9. Death, healing, damage, and reset behavior work correctly.
10. The UI does not maintain a duplicate authoritative health state.
11. Event subscriptions are correctly managed.
12. Existing combat logging functionality remains unaffected.
13. No Unity Console errors occur.

## Out of scope

Do not implement floating damage numbers, healing numbers, shield overlays, cast bars, resource bars, buffs, debuffs, advanced health bar animations, or multi-boss HUD management.

## Deliverables

Implement the necessary C# components, prefabs, UI changes, and appropriate tests.

Summarize the files modified, architectural decisions, and required Unity Editor configuration.
